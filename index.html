About

My research focuses on robust and high-precision control of robot manipulators under external disturbances and model uncertainties, with particular emphasis on adaptive sliding mode control and its integration with neural network-based estimation. I have proposed novel adaptive laws based on quasi-convex functions and averaged sliding variables that improve disturbance robustness while mitigating chattering, and I have developed neural network-assisted time-delay estimation to better compensate for unmodeled dynamics.
