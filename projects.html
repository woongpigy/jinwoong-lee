2024

Home mobility with LLM

A custom ChatGPT-4o GPT converts natural-language commands into ROS2 Python nodes. An API then automatically extracts, packages, builds, and runs the generated code to drive a WeGo LIMO mobile robot. The pipeline was verified with commands such as driving a 2 m-diameter circle twice.

Jinwoong Lee, Hyuk Mo An, Dong Hee Seo · Advisor: Professor Seok Young Lee · Industry partner: Assemble Inc.

  • LLM
  • ROS2
  • Mobile robot
  • ChatGPT-4o

2022

ROS-based precision-control object loading system

An Orbbec Astra 3D depth camera and body tracking let an operator control a ROBOTIS OpenMANIPULATOR-X (4-DOF arm with gripper) with their left arm. ROS nodes turn the left shoulder and hand-tip coordinates into manipulator coordinates, solve inverse kinematics for the joint angles, and open or close the gripper with the operator’s hand to pick up and place objects.

Team Apocalypse: Hyuk Mo An, Jinwoong Lee, Dong Hee Seo · Advisor: Professor Seok Young Lee
